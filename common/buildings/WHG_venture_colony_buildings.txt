###############################################################################
# Colony buildings of the Venturers (power venturer_colony_works): the colony
# structures of the Lustrian frontier. Built in the holdings of a chartered
# colony (province flag venture_colony, from the Colonial Charter war or the
# colonies of 2010, venture.5) by a Venturer of rank 3 or by a lord under one.
# Farmsteads feed the colony and come before a Port, a Monument or a Venue.
# A Capitol needs all three, the colony's established reputation, and
# Fortifications. Yearly colony events read them (venture.50 - 66).
# Triggers are in province scope, FROM is the holder (see 00_Castle.txt).
###############################################################################
