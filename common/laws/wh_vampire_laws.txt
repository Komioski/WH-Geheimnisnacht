###############################################################################
# The Blood Tax
# How hard a vampire ruler bleeds the living of the realm. Light taxes
# please mortal vassals and starve the vampire ones; heavy taxes fill the
# levies with the drained and raised, and stir up revolt. The Jade and the
# Mahtmasi feed in their own ways and have no blood tax. Vampires keep the
# creature_human trait, so their opinion below adds to the mortal one.
# Hidden vampires levy no blood tax: it would give them away.
# Based on Komi's DoW-PoD blood tax. Loc: localisation/00_WHG_undead_events.csv
###############################################################################
