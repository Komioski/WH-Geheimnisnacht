###############################################################################
# WHG Religious Orders
#
# Temple orders and priesthoods, whatever the faction:
#   Handmaidens of the Lady (Bretonnia), the Fellowship of the Shroud (Morr),
#   the Order of the Bleeding Heart (Shallya), the Order of Mysteries (Verena)
#   and the Amazon Sisterhood (Lustria).
#
# All are is_religious = yes and indestructible, and every startup_populate
# checks is_in_society = no. Morr, Shallya and Verena also keep starting members
# inside the Old World, Araby or the Badlands, so nobody far away is pulled in
# just for scoring well; the Handmaidens and Amazons are gated by faith instead.
# Witch hunters keep to their own orders (see WHG_witch_hunter_societies.txt).
###############################################################################
