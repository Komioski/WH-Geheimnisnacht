###############################################################################
# WHG Warrior Lodges
#
# Every martial brotherhood in the mod lives here, whatever the faction:
#   Knights of Bretonnia, the Knightly Orders of the Old World, the Warriors of
#   Ulric, the Circle of the Bear (Kislev), Norscan warriors, the five Chaos
#   warbands, the Gork and Mork warbands, the Dawi Warriors Guild and the
#   Skaven Warlords.
#
# Rules every lodge here follows:
#   - indestructible = yes always comes with a startup_populate, and that
#     startup_populate checks is_in_society = no. Without it the engine fills
#     ranks (and picks a new head) from the best-scoring character anywhere,
#     pulling them out of their own society.
#   - can_join/potential gate by religion and creature trait, so a lodge only
#     shows to the people it belongs to.
#   - Currency lines (triggered_gain) need a CURR* loc key in the faction's
#     localisation file.
#
# Circle of the Bear (Kislev): rank-2 members may take a posting in the Rota of
# the Dawn, Dazh's temple guard. Events: events/WHG_kislev_events.txt
# (kislevsoc); decisions: decisions/WHG_kislev_society_decisions.txt;
# triggers/effects: wh_kislev_society_triggers.txt, wh_kislev_society_effects.txt.
# Borrows interface/wh_kislev_societies.gfx art until its own is made.
###############################################################################
