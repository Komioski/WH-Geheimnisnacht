# Antagonize
# Namespace: antagonize (was HF.22000 - HF.22999 in HF_antagonize_events;
#   antagonize.N = old HF.22N, so HF.22999 is antagonize.999)
# Faction: any landed ruler (Antagonize decision; the AI uses sway.997/998)
#
# A ruler who sets out to antagonize another torments them for years:
# slander about their traits and sins, a hired blackguard in their court,
# poisoned gifts that play on their vices, spies and counter-spies. Each
# pulse rolls one attempt; the victim answers with forgiveness, contempt, a
# duel or bitter hatred, and enough of it makes them a rival. Warhammer
# slanders: a learned rival painted as a witch, the taint of mutation,
# unlicensed magic, and the old hatred between Dawi and elgi. Now and then
# the blackguard asks to stay on, bringing his cutthroats or his talents.
#
# Yearly driver                          antagonize.999, antagonize.0
# Clean-up (prison, incapable, rival,    antagonize.2 - antagonize.11,
#   friend, death)                         antagonize.119 - antagonize.123
# Open insults                           antagonize.12 - antagonize.19
# Slander about traits and sins          antagonize.20 - antagonize.49
#   (topic pick 20, flag clean-up 40/41, outcomes 30 - 49)
# The hired blackguard                   antagonize.50 - antagonize.68,
#                                          antagonize.134
# Poisoned gifts for the vices           antagonize.69 - antagonize.110
#   (courtesan, feast, forged crown, false survey, ruined parade,
#   false claim, rotting portrait)
# Reactions and side effects             antagonize.111 - antagonize.118
# Rival campaigns (rumours, spies)       antagonize.120 - antagonize.130
# Lunatic victims                        antagonize.131 - antagonize.133
#
# Saved: persistent event target antagonize_target; character flags
#   flag_character_being_antagonized, flag_antagonized_option_* (slander
#   topic, cleared by antagonize_clear_option_flags_effect),
#   flag_antagonize_slander_*, antagonize_kept_blackguard (once per life),
#   antagonize_blackguard_agent; unit earmark antagonize_cutthroats.
# Support: common/scripted_effects/wh_antagonize_effects.txt,
#   random_antagonizer_slander_roll_effect (00_scripted_effects.txt),
#   GetRandomSlanderDesc / GetRandomSlanderReactionDesc (customizable loc).
# Loc: localisation/00_WHG_antagonize_events.csv
