###############################################################################
# Magic gift events
# Namespace: gift (was magicpotential in S_magic_inheritance_events.txt)
# Faction: Generic
#
# The gift of magic: who is born with it, how it shows when a child comes of
# age, whether he hides it, and what his lord does when he finds out. In
# lands that burn witches, mages living openly are eventually exposed. The
# Ice Witches hunt men who touch the winds in Kislev (the Damsels do the
# same in Bretonnia), and Arabyan scholars hunt women who study magic.
#
# The gift is hidden (magic_potential_flag_1-4) until it shows, then becomes
# the magic_potential_1-4 trait; 4 is the strongest. A recessive_mage flag
# marks a carrier who can pass the gift on but never wield it.
#
# Born with the gift                     gift.1
# The gift shows                         gift.2 - gift.9
# Exposed                                gift.10 - gift.11
# The Ice Witches' judgement             gift.20 - gift.24
# The scholars of Araby                  gift.30 - gift.35
#
# The witch hunters' own hunt for mages is whunter.150 - whunter.152 in
# WHG_witch_hunter_events.txt (it replaced magicpotential.7 - 9).
#
# Callers:
#   common/on_actions/wh_on_actions.txt: gift.1 on on_birth, gift.2 on
#     on_adulthood, gift.10 and gift.30 in the magic_gift_exposure list on
#     on_five_year_pulse.
#   common/scripted_effects/wh_assign_racial_trait.txt: new_character_effect
#     fires gift.1 for some generated characters.
#   events/S_spellsinger_events.txt: spellsinger.16 fires gift.3.
#   events/WHG_kislev_events.txt (kislev.7) and events/wh_bretonnia_events.txt
#     (bretonmagic.4) fire gift.20; the verdict goes back as kislev.8/9 or
#     bretonmagic.5/6.
# Scripted triggers: common/scripted_triggers/wh_magic_gift_triggers.txt
# Scripted effects: common/scripted_effects/wh_magic_gift_effects.txt
# Loc: localisation/00_WHG_magic_gift_events.csv
###############################################################################
