###############################################################################
# Rumours at court (namespace rumour)
# Namespace: rumour (was 69000 - 69102 in rumours_events.txt; rumour.N =
#   old id minus 68900)
# Faction: Generic
# Serves: every court of the Old World and Araby
#
# A scheming courtier or ruler whispers about someone at court: a sodomite,
# an incestuous brother, an impotent husband, an adulterer, an occultist, a
# useless councillor, an impious soul or a coward. The whisperer may
# confront the target, spread the rumour (every courtier then thinks less of
# them) or blackmail them for fifty gold. Spread rumours stick as character
# flags and bring their own trouble: the brothel, the priest's sermon, the
# child who saw too much, the scolding from the head of the faith, the
# women laughing at a coward.
#
#   rumour.100 - 108  The sodomite
#   rumour.110 - 117  Incest with a sister
#   rumour.120 - 124  Impotence
#   rumour.130 - 139  Adultery
#   rumour.140 - 147  The occultist
#   rumour.150 - 159  The useless councillor (one pair per council seat)
#   rumour.160 - 166  Lack of piety; the Grand Theogonist's envoy for
#                     Sigmarites (165), the Sultan's scolding in Araby (166)
#   rumour.170 - 176  The coward
#   rumour.180 - 191  Everyone has heard (the notifications)
#   rumour.195 - 202  The court priest and lack of piety in Araby
#
# Fired from: on_yearly_pulse in 00_on_actions.txt (the Rumours list, the
#   whisperers rumour.100, 103, 110, 114, 120, 123, 130, 137, 140, 143, 150,
#   152, 154, 156, 158, 160, 163, 170, 173, 200, 195), and the yearly list
#   proving_bravery in wh_on_actions.txt (rumour.174, 176). The follow-ups
#   are MTTH events behind the rumour's own flag as a pre-trigger.
# Fixed: rumour.174 and 176 (a coward proving his bravery) were MTTH events
#   on every character in the world; they are a yearly list now. Denying the
#   coward rumour set a flag nothing read (suspected_coward), now
#   flag_suspected_coward like the rest of the chain. The priest's talk
#   about incest (rumour.115) had an Araby branch its religion pre-trigger
#   never let through; Araby faiths now get it too. Targets are checked with
#   is_woman_trigger, and the romantic rumours need a race with women. The
#   impiety flags of the two faith groups are one flag, rumour_impious, so
#   the child's question (rumour.164) comes in Araby as well.
# Saved: character flags rumour_spreader, suspected_sodomite,
#   suspected_of_incest, sister_suspected_of_incest, impotent_man,
#   committed_adultery, suspected_occultist, rumour_impious,
#   flag_suspected_coward.
# Loc: localisation/00_WHG_rumour_events.csv (the vanilla texts, with the
#   Church, the Pope, Christians, Muslims, imams, Allah, the Caliph and hell
#   moved into the setting).
###############################################################################
