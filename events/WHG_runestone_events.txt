###############################################################################
# Runestones
# Namespace: runestone (was TOG.500 - TOG.580 in oldgods_runestone_events.txt)
# Faction: Norsca
# Serves: Norscans and dawi (the commission_runestone decision)
#
# A lord of the north raises a carved and painted stone to boast of
# themselves or to remember a dead father or mother; a dawi lord has the
# clan's runesmiths cut it into the hold's ancestor hall. The stone may speak
# of one of the lord's qualities (one choice per trait, as in vanilla), and a
# dawi may instead carve a grudge against a rival into it, shaming the rival
# before all. A raider may boast of the coasts burned in the south. The mad,
# the possessed and the witless get only the stone they deserve.
#
# The stone speaks in the voice of its people: the opening, the closing and
# the lineage line come from WHG_old_gods_voices.txt.
#
# The commission                       runestone.1 - runestone.2
# The stone is raised                  runestone.10
#
# Fired from: the commission_runestone decision in minor_decisions.txt
#   (runestone.1, once per life). TOG.580 (the Norse stopping the practice in
#   the high middle ages) is dropped. Shared triggers and effects are in
#   WHG_old_gods_triggers.txt and WHG_old_gods_effects.txt.
# Saved: event targets runestone_parent, runestone_grudge_target; flags
#   runestone_for_father, runestone_for_mother, runestone_death_*,
#   runestone_self_* (cleared by runestone_clear_flags_effect).
###############################################################################
