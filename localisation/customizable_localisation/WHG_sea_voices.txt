# Voices of the sea for the pirate and raider module (events/WHG_sea_*).
# The voice is read from the character in scope, in this order (see
# common/scripted_triggers/WHG_sea_triggers.txt): Druchii corsairs, the
# Vampire Coast, Orc freebootaz, the Norse, Araby, and Sartosa for every
# other captain. GetSeaTheatre and GetSeaHome name the sea from the
# sea_in_<theatre> and sea_home_<theatre> flags; GetSeaMood reads the
# manann_mood variable.
